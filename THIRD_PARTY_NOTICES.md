# Third-party notices

The production ZIP contains no bundled runtime package, font or third-party image library.
Barcode symbol tables and encoders were inherited from the supplied RC8 source; their
earlier derivation still requires owner attestation. Do not infer proven originality from
the absence of package-manager dependencies. See docs/provenance.md.

Independent test decoders are **not included** in the ZIP:

- zxing-wasm: MIT wrapper, https://github.com/Sec-ant/zxing-wasm; upstream ZXing-C++
  Apache-2.0, https://github.com/zxing-cpp/zxing-cpp. Keep its LICENSE and NOTICE when redistributing it.
- @undecaf/zbar-wasm: LGPL-2.1+ package, https://github.com/undecaf/zbar-wasm, derived from
  ZBar. Its source and license obligations apply to redistribution of that test binary.
- Playwright (Apache-2.0), ESLint and Prettier (MIT), and PHPUnit (BSD-3-Clause) are
  developer tools, with exact transitive versions recorded in the lockfiles.

Installed dependencies retain their own copyright and LICENSE/NOTICE files. Run
`python3 bin/check-licenses.py` after installing dependencies to inspect approved license
metadata, and `composer licenses --format=json` for the PHP inventory. Metadata checks
do not establish RC8 authorship or independently prove source-code provenance.

The documentation's synthetic QR example is generated by this plugin from test data;
it includes no customer data or redistributed font files. LICENSE contains the standard
GNU GPL v2 text; the project grant is GPL-2.0-or-later, subject to the owner's rights confirmation.
